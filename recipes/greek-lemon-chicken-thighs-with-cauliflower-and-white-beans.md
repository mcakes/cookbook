## Method

1. In a large bowl, whisk 2 tablespoons olive oil, garlic, lemon zest, juice of 1 lemon, oregano, 1 teaspoon kosher salt, and several grinds of pepper. Add chicken thighs and toss to coat. Let sit at room temperature 15 minutes (or refrigerate up to 8 hours).
2. Adjust oven rack to upper-middle position and preheat oven to 425°F (220°C).
3. On a 13x18-inch rimmed sheet pan, toss cauliflower florets and cannellini beans with the remaining 2 tablespoons olive oil, 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt, and several grinds of pepper. Spread in a single layer.
4. Nestle chicken thighs skin-side up among the cauliflower and beans. Pour any remaining marinade over the chicken.
5. Roast 30-35 minutes, until chicken skin is deep golden brown and the thickest thigh registers 175°F (80°C) on an instant-read thermometer. Cauliflower should be tender with charred edges.
6. Scatter olives over the pan and return to oven for 2 minutes to warm through.
7. Remove from oven. Drizzle the remaining lemon juice over everything. Top with crumbled feta (if using) and parsley. Serve from the pan.
