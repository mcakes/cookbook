ingredients:
  - 150 g blanched almonds
  - 60 g stale white bread, crusts removed
  - 1 small garlic clove
  - 325 ml ice water
  - 100 ml extra-virgin olive oil
  - 1 tbsp sherry vinegar
  - 1 tsp fine salt
  - 3 tbsp Marcona almonds, toasted and roughly chopped, to serve
  - Flaky salt and best finishing olive oil, to serve
---

## Method

1. Cover the blanched almonds with very hot water and leave to soften for 30 minutes. Tear the bread into pieces and soak in a little cold water at the same time.
2. Drain the almonds and squeeze the water out of the bread.
3. Blend the almonds and bread with the garlic, salt, sherry vinegar and about half the ice water until completely smooth — a full two minutes, longer than feels necessary. Any grit left here will not disappear later.
4. With the blender running, stream in the olive oil until it emulsifies and turns pale and glossy.
5. Thin with the remaining ice water to the consistency of heavy cream. Pass through a fine sieve.
6. Chill hard, at least 1 hour. Taste again when cold — chilling mutes both salt and acid, so it will almost certainly want a little more of each.
7. Serve very cold in shallow bowls, scattered with Marcona almonds and finished with olive oil and flaky salt.

## Notes

Ice water rather than tap keeps the emulsion from breaking as the blender heats up, and gets it closer to serving temperature before it even hits the fridge.

The traditional Andalusian garnish is halved green grapes, which push it sweeter. Melon works the same way.

**Serving with marinated tomatoes.** This was built as the base for [Vinegar-Marinated Cherry Tomatoes](vinegar-marinated-cherry-tomatoes.md) as a summer first course — the almond fat cushions the vinegar without muting it, and sherry vinegar seasons both components, so they read as one dish rather than two stacked. Pool about 120 ml per shallow bowl, arrange the tomatoes and salted cucumber on top, spoon over a little of the tomato marinade, then finish with herbs, Marcona almonds, olive oil and flaky salt. Both recipes serve 4 as written. The Marcona almonds matter more in the composed version than they do here — everything else on that plate is soft.
