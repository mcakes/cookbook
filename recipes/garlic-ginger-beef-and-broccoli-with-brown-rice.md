## Method

1. Cook brown rice according to package directions (typically 1 cup rice + 2 1/4 cups water, simmered covered 40-45 minutes, then rested 10 minutes off heat). Start first.
2. In a medium bowl, toss sliced beef with 1 1/2 tablespoons tamari, 2 teaspoons cornstarch, and toasted sesame oil. Let marinate 10 minutes at room temperature.
3. In a small bowl, whisk broth, the remaining 3 tablespoons tamari, rice vinegar, oyster sauce, the remaining 1 1/2 teaspoons cornstarch, and red pepper flakes (if using). Set aside.
4. Bring a medium pot of water to a boil. Blanch broccoli 90 seconds, drain in a colander, and shake out excess water.
5. Heat 1 1/2 tablespoons neutral oil in a 14-inch wok or 12-inch heavy skillet over high heat until smoking. Add half the beef in a single layer. Cook undisturbed 60 seconds, then stir-fry until just browned, about 1 minute more. Transfer to a plate. Repeat with another 1 1/2 tablespoons oil and the remaining beef.
6. Reduce heat to medium-high. Add the remaining oil if the pan is dry. Add garlic and ginger. Stir-fry 20 seconds, until fragrant.
7. Add broccoli and stir-fry 1 minute. Whisk sauce again to recombine, then pour into the pan. Stir-fry until sauce coats and thickens, 30-60 seconds.
8. Return beef and any accumulated juices to the pan. Toss to coat and heat through, 30 seconds.
9. Serve over brown rice. Top with scallions and sesame seeds.
