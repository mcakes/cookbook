  - 6 heads baby bok choy (~1 lb / 450 g total), halved lengthwise
  - 1 tablespoon toasted sesame seeds
  - 3 scallions, thinly sliced (white and green parts kept separate)

## Method

1. Cook brown rice according to package directions (typically 1 cup rice + 2 1/4 cups water, simmered covered 40-45 minutes, then rested 10 minutes off heat). Start this first so it finishes near the end.
2. Drain tofu and press: wrap each block in a clean towel, set on a plate, top with a heavy pan or a few cans, and press 10 minutes. Cut each block into 3/4-inch (2 cm) cubes.
3. In a medium bowl, whisk gochujang, tamari, rice vinegar, sesame oil, maple syrup, half the grated garlic, and the ginger. Set aside.
4. Pat tofu cubes dry once more with paper towels, then toss with cornstarch in a large bowl until evenly coated.
5. Heat 2 tablespoons neutral oil in a 12-inch nonstick or well-seasoned cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. Add tofu in a single layer (work in two batches if needed). Cook undisturbed 3-4 minutes per side, turning until 3-4 sides are deep golden and crisp, 10-12 minutes total. Transfer to a plate.
6. Reduce heat to medium. Add the remaining 1 tablespoon oil, scallion whites, and remaining garlic. Cook 30 seconds, until fragrant.
7. Add bok choy cut-side down. Cook undisturbed 2-3 minutes, until charred. Flip, add 2 tablespoons water, cover, and steam 2 minutes more, until stems are just tender. Transfer to serving plates or a platter.
8. Return tofu to the skillet over medium heat. Pour in gochujang sauce and toss gently to coat, cooking until sauce thickens and glazes the tofu, 1-2 minutes.
9. Serve tofu and bok choy over brown rice. Top with sesame seeds and scallion greens.
