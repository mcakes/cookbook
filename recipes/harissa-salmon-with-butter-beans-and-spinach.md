## Method

1. Adjust oven rack to upper-middle position and preheat oven to 425°F (220°C).
2. In a small bowl, stir together harissa, 1 tablespoon olive oil, and lemon zest. Pat salmon dry, then rub the harissa mixture all over the flesh side. Set aside on a plate.
3. On a 13x18-inch rimmed sheet pan, toss butter beans, cherry tomatoes, sliced garlic, cumin, 2 tablespoons olive oil, 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt, and several grinds of pepper. Spread in an even layer.
4. Roast bean mixture 12 minutes, until tomatoes have started to burst and beans are warmed through.
5. Remove pan from oven and gently push the bean mixture aside to make four spaces. Place salmon fillets skin-side down in the spaces.
6. Return to oven and roast 10-13 minutes, until salmon is just opaque at the thickest part (internal temp 120-125°F / 49-52°C for medium).
7. Remove from oven. Squeeze juice from the half lemon over the pan, then pile baby spinach on top of the bean mixture (around the salmon) and toss with tongs until wilted in the residual heat.
8. Taste beans and spinach for salt and acid; adjust as needed. Scatter parsley over the top and serve with lemon wedges.
