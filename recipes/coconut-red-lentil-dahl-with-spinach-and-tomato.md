## Method

1. Heat oil in a large heavy pot or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add onion and a pinch of salt. Cook, stirring occasionally, until softened and lightly golden, 6-8 minutes.
2. Add garlic, ginger, and chili (if using). Cook, stirring, until fragrant, 1 minute.
3. Add garam masala, cumin, turmeric, and coriander. Toast in the oil, stirring constantly, 30-60 seconds, until fragrant.
4. Add lentils, diced tomatoes (with juice), coconut milk, water, and 1 1/4 teaspoons kosher salt. Stir well, scraping up any spice paste from the bottom.
5. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low. Simmer uncovered, stirring occasionally to prevent sticking, until lentils have broken down into a thick, creamy purée, 18-22 minutes. If it gets too thick, splash in a little more water.
6. Stir in spinach a handful at a time, letting each addition wilt before adding the next.
7. Off heat, stir in lemon juice. Taste and adjust salt and acid.
8. Ladle into bowls and top with cilantro. Serve as-is, or with a small portion of brown rice or wholemeal flatbread.
