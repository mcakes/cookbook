## Method

1. Adjust oven rack to upper-middle position and preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
2. Heat olive oil in a 12-inch oven-safe skillet (cast-iron or stainless) over medium heat. Add onion and bell peppers with a pinch of salt. Cook, stirring occasionally, until very soft and lightly caramelized at the edges, 10-12 minutes.
3. Add garlic, smoked paprika, cumin, and Aleppo pepper. Cook, stirring, 1 minute.
4. Stir in tomato paste and cook 1 minute, until darkened and fragrant.
5. Add crushed tomatoes and 3/4 teaspoon kosher salt. Bring to a simmer and cook, stirring occasionally, until the sauce has thickened and pulls away from the side of the pan when stirred, 8-10 minutes.
6. Stir in kale a handful at a time, letting each addition wilt before adding the next. Simmer 2 minutes, until kale is tender. Taste sauce and adjust salt and pepper.
7. Use the back of a spoon to make 8 wells in the sauce. Crack one egg into each well. Sprinkle eggs lightly with salt and pepper.
8. Transfer skillet to oven. Bake 8-12 minutes, until whites are just set and yolks are still soft. (Or cover skillet on the stovetop over low heat for similar timing.)
9. Scatter feta and parsley over the top, drizzle with a little more olive oil, and serve directly from the pan.
